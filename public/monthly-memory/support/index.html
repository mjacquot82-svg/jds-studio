Help & support

Questions about your prints, your plan, a delivery, or the app? Email us and a real person at JDS Studio will reply, usually within 2 business days.

contact@jdsstudio.ca

In the app you can also tap Account, then Contact support.

To help us help you, include

  • the name and mailing address on your account;
  • which month or delivery it is about;
  • for damaged or missing prints, a photo of the problem (within 30 days of delivery);
  • your phone model, if it is about the app.

Please never email passwords or full card numbers.

Quick answers

  • Pay: Interac e-Transfer of the amount shown in Account, Plan to mjacquot82@gmail.com, with your name or account code in the message, before the 3rd of the month. Unpaid months are held until payment arrives.
  • Skip a month: Account, Plan, Skip next month, before the cutoff. Skipped months are free and you can undo.
  • Change your plan or address: Account, Plan or Delivery address.
  • Send a copy to family: Account, Share with family.
  • Cancel: Account, Plan, Cancel subscription. Future months stop. You can also email us.
