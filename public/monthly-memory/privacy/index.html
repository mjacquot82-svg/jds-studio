MONTHLY MEMORY

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Monthly Memory is built so that your photo library stays on your phone. This policy explains, in plain language, what JDS Studio ("we") collects, why, and your choices. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

The short version
  • Your photo library, the faces you add, and the app's face matching never leave your phone.
  • Only the finished, cropped prints for each month and the mailing addresses are sent, so they can be printed and mailed.
  • No ads, no selling or renting your information, no tracking or analytics in the app.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Your photo library. The app reads photos only from the months it is preparing, with the permission you give.
  • Reference faces for the people you add, and the face matching used to find them. These are stored encrypted in the app's private storage on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • Your saved monthly collections, crops and choices, stored encrypted on your phone.
  • App backups to the cloud are turned off for this data, so it is not copied to your Google account by the app.

Deleting the app removes this data from your phone.

2. What we receive

When a month is confirmed, the app sends to the JDS Studio order server:

  • the finished prints for that month: copies of only the selected photos, already cropped to the print size;
  • your name and mailing address, and the name and mailing address of each Send-to recipient you add;
  • your plan (print size and number of prints), the month, and an order reference;
  • your subscription status: a random subscription number created by the app, whether you are active or cancelled, which months you skipped, your plan and its price. It contains no photos, names or addresses and is used only to make sure skipped or cancelled months are never printed or charged;
  • your email address and payment details if and when you give them to us for billing. Card details, if used, are handled by a payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write or attach.

3. Why we use it

Only to print and mail your prints, bill for the service, answer your questions, keep records the law requires, and keep the service secure. We do not use your photos or addresses for advertising, and we do not use your photos to train any artificial intelligence.

4. Who we share it with

  • Our Canadian photo lab partner, which prints the photos and mails them. It receives the prints, the recipient names and mailing addresses, and an order reference.
  • Canada Post, through the mailing label.
  • Service providers that run our systems for us: cloud hosting for the order server, which is located in the United States, and an email service we use to send order notices to ourselves. Information stored or processed outside Canada may be accessible to courts and authorities in that country under its laws.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. How long we keep it

  • Print copies on the order server are deleted automatically 14 days after the order is finished.
  • Order records (names, mailing addresses, order references and the lab's order confirmation) are kept while your account is active and then as long as needed for billing, tax and accounting records (generally up to 7 years), then deleted.

6. Security

The app talks to the order server only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and uses a device key. Data on your phone is encrypted in the app's private storage. Access to order records is limited to JDS Studio. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can see, correct or ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you, and withdraw your consent at any time (which may mean we can no longer send prints). Email contact@jdsstudio.ca. We will answer within 30 days.
  • You can remove photo access, delete people or Send-to recipients, or delete the app at any time.
  • If you add a Send-to recipient, please make sure they are comfortable with us having their name and address for mailing prints. They can contact us directly to be removed.

8. Children

Monthly Memory accounts are for adults. Your prints may include your children or family; those photos are handled under this policy like any other photo you choose to print.

9. Changes

We will post updates here and change the date at the top. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app or by email first.

10. Contact and complaints

Privacy questions or requests: JDS Studio, Walkerton, Ontario, contact@jdsstudio.ca (attention: privacy). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
