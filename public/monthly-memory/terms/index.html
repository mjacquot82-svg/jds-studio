1. What Monthly Memory does

Each month the app helps you pick a small set of your own photos, mostly of the people you choose. The sorting, face matching and cropping happen on your phone. When your month is confirmed, the finished prints are sent to us and printed by a Canadian photo lab partner, then mailed to the addresses you gave. Monthly Memory is delivery only; there is no in-store pickup.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 (or the age of majority where you live) and live in Canada. Prints are mailed only to Canadian addresses.

4. Skipping and cancelling

  • Skip a month for free, in the app. Tap Account, Plan, Skip next month (or Account, Skip a month) before that month's cutoff (normally the end of the 3rd day of the next month). A skipped month is not printed and not charged, and you can undo the skip until the cutoff.
  • Cancel any time, in the app. Tap Account, Plan, Cancel subscription and confirm. Cancelling stops all future months: nothing more is collected, printed or charged. Your photos, people and saved collections stay on your phone, and you can restart later from the same screen. A month that has already been sent for printing cannot be cancelled.
  • You can change your plan in the app (Account, Plan) at any time.
  • If you cannot use the app, email contact@jdsstudio.ca to skip or cancel and we will do it for you.
